Outfit AI

Privacy Policy

Overview

Outfit AI ("the app") helps you build outfit combinations from clothing items you add yourself. Your privacy matters, and the app is designed to work entirely on your device.

Information We Collect

We do not collect, transmit, or share any personal information. The clothing items and saved outfits you create are stored only in a local database on your device.

Internet, Accounts and Permissions

The app does not require an account, does not connect to any server or API, and does not request special device permissions such as camera, location, contacts, or storage.

Third-Party Services

The app does not include advertising, analytics, or tracking services from third parties.

Data Deletion

You can delete any item or outfit inside the app at any time. Uninstalling the app removes all locally stored data from your device.

Children's Privacy

The app does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
